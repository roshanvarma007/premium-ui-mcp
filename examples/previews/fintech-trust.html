Review checklist

  • Does it feel trustworthy, calm, precise, credible, professional, serious? Would it fit: banking, payments, investing, insurance, accounting?
  • Is the dark theme its own design (not an inversion), with comfortable contrast?
  • Is there exactly one accent, and do semantic colors read as meaning only?
  • Do display and body faces pair well at every size? Are money values steady (tabular)?
  • Is anything generic, glossy, or reminiscent of a specific brand?

Signature details

  • Tabular numerals for all money values
  • Hairline dividers instead of boxes
  • Serif display only for page titles and hero balances; everything else in the sans
  • Generous top-of-screen whitespace before the key number
  • Muted currency symbols and decimals so the integer part leads

Avoid

  • gradients
  • blur / glassmorphism
  • glow shadows
  • more than one accent color
  • pure #000 on pure #FFF
  • default Material purple
  • navy and cyan combinations
  • red and green as the only signal (pair with signs and icons)
  • animated counters on balances
  • stock photos of handshakes or coins
